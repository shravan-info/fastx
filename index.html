Your privacy matters

Fastx does not require an account and does not add analytics or advertising trackers. Recent searches are saved only in this browser so you can revisit them. Search suggestions and knowledge results are requested from DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia when you use those features.

Full web, image, video, news, book and map searches open the external provider you select. Its own privacy policy applies there.
